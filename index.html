Tips

  • Punch builds power; kick and special spend it, everything else is free
  • Crouch ducks kicks and high specials (bolt, high kick)
  • Ground attacks (rat rush, sweep kick, slides) can only be jumped
  • Block stops punch/kick but not specials or slides
  • Uppercut punishes a jump-in up close
  • Controllers work too - plug one in and press any button, no setup needed. Menus, character select, and match exit/restart are all controller-navigable (D-pad/stick + A to confirm, B to go back).
